          How it works

          What happens when I press “Check”

          The text and the source you picked are sent to the Phish-Catch API for this one request. The API returns a 0–100 scam-likelihood score, the warning signs it found, and an estimate of what could be affected if you did what the message asks. Nothing is stored as history.

          AI analysis and the local rules

          Messages are analysed by an AI model through Vercel AI Gateway, or by Google Gemini or an OpenAI-compatible model when the site owner configures one. The model reads the message, decides how likely it is to be a scam, and explains the red flags and possible impact. If AI analysis is unavailable, turned off, or fails, a local rule-based engine is used instead. The results card always says which one produced your result.

          Some requests — sharing an OTP, entering a UPI PIN, giving a password, installing remote-access apps, “digital arrest” threats — always raise the score, whatever the model says.

          What the rules look for

          Requests for OTPs, PINs, passwords and ID numbers; links that use shorteners, unusual domain endings or brand names on unofficial domains; KYC and account-blocking threats; prizes, upfront fees, task jobs and guaranteed returns; fake police or courier stories; secrecy, urgency, threats, and requests to meet or share your location. The source you choose adjusts the weighting — for example, a code request on WhatsApp weighs more on account takeover.

          Voice notes

          Transcription is a separate step that needs a speech provider configured by the site owner. The transcript is placed in the message box so you can listen back and correct it before checking. If transcription isn’t available, play the recording and type what it says.

          Reading the score and impact levels

          0–29 Safe no strong scam signals. 30–59 Caution some warning signs; verify before acting. 60–100 Scam strong scam signals; don’t engage.

          Impact levels (none, low, medium, high) estimate the risk to your device, finances, social accounts and physical safety if you followed the message’s instructions.

          Limitations

          Results are estimates, not proof that a message is genuine or fraudulent. Phish-Catch can’t verify the sender, open links, scan your device, access your accounts, confirm transactions or contact authorities. New scam styles can be missed and legitimate messages can be flagged. When in doubt, contact the organisation using details you find yourself.

          Privacy: messages and recordings are processed for a single check and not kept as history by Phish-Catch. For AI analysis, your text is sent to the AI provider for that check, and the provider’s own data policy applies (free AI tiers may use inputs to improve their products), so remove personal details first.

          Safety: results are estimates. In immediate danger call 112. For cyber financial fraud call 1930 or visit cybercrime.gov.in.
